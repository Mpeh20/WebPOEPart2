About CTARS

Rescuing, rehabilitating and rehoming animals since 2005.

Our History

CTARS was founded in 2005 by a small group of Cape Town animal lovers who began rescuing stray and abused dogs and cats from the streets. What started as a weekend volunteer effort has grown into a recognised non-profit organisation with a network of foster homes, a central adoption centre, and hundreds of animals rehomed every year.

For nearly two decades, CTARS has remained entirely volunteer-run and donation-funded. Every rand goes directly towards veterinary care, food, shelter and rehoming efforts.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned or abused animals in Cape Town, while promoting responsible pet ownership and compassion towards all living creatures.

Our Vision

A compassionate Cape Town where every animal is treated with dignity, has access to proper care, and finds a loving forever home.
