Our Services

From rescue to rehoming — what we do, and how you can adopt.

What We Offer

Rescue & Rehabilitation

We rescue abandoned, abused and injured animals, and provide full veterinary care, sterilisation and rehabilitation before rehoming.

Adoption & Rehoming

We match every animal with a carefully screened forever home. Meet-and-greets and home checks ensure the best fit for both pet and family.

Foster Care Programme

Foster families give animals a safe, loving home environment while they wait for adoption — we provide food, vet care and support.

Community Education

We run school visits, community workshops and awareness campaigns about responsible pet ownership, sterilisation and animal welfare.

Sterilisation Outreach

Free or subsidised sterilisation drives in under-served communities to reduce the number of unwanted litters.

Food & Supply Support

We distribute pet food and supplies to vulnerable pet owners, keeping animals with their families wherever possible.

How Adoption Works

  1. Browse & enquire: Find a pet you connect with and submit an enquiry.
  2. Meet & greet: We arrange a meeting at our centre or via a foster home.
  3. Home check: A volunteer visits your home to ensure a safe environment.
  4. Adoption fee: A contribution covers sterilisation, vaccination and microchipping.
  5. Welcome home: You take your new family member home. We follow up at 1 month and 6 months.
