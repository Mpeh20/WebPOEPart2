CTARS rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes abandoned animals in Cape Town. Together, we can build a future where every pet has a loving home.

Our Mission

To rescue, heal, and find loving forever homes for every animal in our care, while promoting responsible pet ownership through education and community engagement.

Our Vision

A world where every companion animal is cherished, safe, and part of a caring family – no animal left behind.
